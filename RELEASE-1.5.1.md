## Mini Outline Refinements

- Group Reading shortcuts above Mini outline in settings. Keep everyday choices visible and put placement, depth, transparency, double-click and hover preferences under More outline options. Rename the document-edge entry to Mini outline button and clarify Floating TOC coexistence.
- Set the outline width from 260 to 600 pixels, with a responsive default and narrow-pane limits. Choose one to four heading lines, defaulting to two; reveal a full title from its menu. Clean Markdown styling only in labels, without changing source text or navigation.
- Use a lighter icon bar and an optional transparent desktop background. Controls appear on mouse hover or keyboard focus; an idle pinned outline retains the pin indicator. Fix persistent controls on mouse-first computers that also report touch capability. Touch controls remain visible.
- Optionally open and pin the active outline after workspace restoration, without the side button, starting speech or moving the document. This defaults to off. Manual dismissal prevents reopening during that session; enabling it may require initial analysis of the active PDF, but never scans the vault or background tabs.
- Add document top/bottom navigation for Markdown, PDFs and local HTML. Separate navigation from speech; an optional, default-off mouse double-click can start reading from a section. Unpinning closes the outline; Close remains in Options, and touch layouts retain a visible Close button.
- Keep the directory clear of reading and Editing Toolbar controls, including after toolbar resizing. Preserve existing preferences and do not modify Floating TOC or other plugins.
