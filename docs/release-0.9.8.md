# 0.9.8 - Model-specific price references

- OpenRouter settings show dated price references for the four ElevenLabs presets and verified MAI models, with per-million and per-10,000-character costs where applicable.
- The ElevenLabs launch offer has an explicit deadline. Settings rendered after it ends show only the last checked list price and a reminder to verify current pricing.
- Unknown models show no numeric estimate. The official-pricing button follows the selected model; price hints make no background requests and never send note text or keys.
- English and Chinese copy distinguishes reference prices from actual charges and warns about repeat synthesis, fees and taxes.
- MAI voice guidance now appears only for MAI models. ElevenLabs has its own guidance and matching voice placeholder.

- Playback, privacy consent and ZDR routing are unchanged. No paid synthesis or new real-device iOS test was performed for this documentation/settings update.
