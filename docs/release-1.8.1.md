# Cozy Read Aloud 1.8.1

- The PDF outline recognizes centered Roman-numbered chapters and lettered subsections even when they have the same size as body text. Wrapped headings keep their first-line jump target.
- Smart reading skips a complex numbered equation block once. Numeric citations on their own line reach the citation reader instead of being spoken as brackets.
- In supported text PDFs, a verified overbar is spoken as “bar”; strict and inclusive comparisons stay distinct. Selected-text reading never widens a selection to recover a mark.
- The update does not edit PDFs, change existing preferences or require OCR. The optional OCR helper is unchanged.
