# A quiet afternoon

## 1. Settle into a chapter

Find a comfortable place and choose a few pages. Let the opening paragraph set the pace. A short reading break is enough.

## 2. Follow a new idea

An interesting sentence can lead to a new question. Give the thought a little room before moving on. Sometimes it helps to hear the words while keeping the page in view. A short pause can make a familiar idea feel fresh again. You can return to the same passage whenever you are ready, without rushing to the next chapter.

### 2.1 Notice the small details

A well-chosen example can make a difficult idea easier to understand. Notice how the writer moves from a question to an explanation. Look for the detail that changes your understanding, then connect it with something you already know.

## 3. Leave room for a pause

Take a moment to reflect on what you have read. You may remember a phrase, a question, or a connection to another book. Write a short note if it helps, then continue when you feel ready. A pause is part of reading, not a failure to keep going.

## 4. Return tomorrow

End at a place that is easy to find again. A small bookmark or a clear heading can help. The next reading break can begin with a quick look back before you explore something new.
